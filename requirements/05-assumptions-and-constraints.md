# 05 · Assumptions & Constraints

We cannot interview the estate, so every assumption is stated here with what changes if it turns out wrong.

## Given constraints (from the brief)
- Wi-Fi coverage on the estate is patchy.
- Cloud services may be used; a way to get data from the estate to the cloud is our problem.
- There is budget for MQTT-capable hardware throughout the park.
- 40 rides, 55 enclosures, 200+ animals, ~5,000 visitors/day today, ≥15,000 target in 3 years.

## Assumptions

## Out of scope
- Ride control systems and their certification. **Condition monitoring stops short of both**: S8 flags a ride for inspection and a certified human decides, on top of an unchanged statutory schedule ([ADR-0022](../adrs/ADR-0022-ride-condition-monitoring.md)). A model may not influence a certification, even advisorily — FR-3.6/3.7's tier-0/tier-1 split has no equivalent here, because a ride has no tier-1.
- **Vehicle autonomy.** The land train of FR-1.9 keeps its driver. A driverless platform is a safety case with five named preconditions before it is a feature, and none of them holds today ([ADR-0020](../adrs/ADR-0020-internal-transport-and-autonomy.md) §4).
- **Remaining-useful-life estimates for rides.** No fleet to fit a distribution against, and a days-until-failure number is the output most easily mistaken for a safety claim.
- Payroll and HR beyond importing staff/skills/availability and exporting staffing plans (A12).
- Physical security (CCTV for theft) — only animal enclosure cameras are in scope.
- Per-animal re-identification in group enclosures (A11), and per-person pricing.
