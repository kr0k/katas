# 07 · Risks & Mitigations

| # | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Uplink to cloud unavailable for hours/days | High | Medium | Edge-first design; ≥ 24 h buffering; gates and safety alerts fully local ([ADR-0001](../adrs/ADR-0001-edge-first-store-and-forward.md)) | Platform |
| R2 | Vision model misses a sick animal (false negative) | Medium | High | Conservative thresholds; sensor-based rules (feed scale, water) as independent signal; daily keeper walk remains; recall tracked on golden set ([ADR-0007](../adrs/ADR-0007-human-in-the-loop-confidence-bands.md)) | Welfare |
| R3 | Too many false alarms → vet ignores the queue | Medium | High | Confidence bands; override rate as KPI (OKR 3.5); threshold recalibration loop | Welfare |
| R4 | LLM provider raises prices or shuts down | Medium | Medium | Inference gateway; per-capability budgets; named fallback provider; open-weight downgrade path on managed hosting ([ADR-0005](../adrs/ADR-0005-model-gateway-and-provider-independence.md)) | AI platform |
| R5 | Guest companion gives wrong/unsafe info (e.g. "you may touch the frog") | Medium | High | Grounding on estate knowledge base only; safety-critical facts served from structured data, not generated; refusal policy; eval suite with adversarial cases ([ADR-0010](../adrs/ADR-0010-grounded-llm-with-guardrails.md)) | Guest |
| R6 | Visitor tracking perceived as surveillance | Medium | High | Anonymous counting only; no face recognition; visitor areas masked in enclosure cameras; transparent signage ([ADR-0009](../adrs/ADR-0009-visitor-privacy-anonymous-counting.md)) | Platform |
| R7 | Forecasts unreliable in year 1 (no history) | High | Low | Heuristic staffing rules until data accumulates; model promoted only when MAPE target met | Ops |
| R8 | Dynamic pricing perceived as unfair | Medium | Medium | Hard floors/ceilings; transparent "quiet-day discount" framing; never raise price after a family has started checkout ([S5](../hld/scenarios/dynamic-family-passes/README.md)) | Guest |
| R9 | Small team cannot operate the platform | Medium | High | Managed cloud services; GitOps; one event backbone; no self-hosted model serving in cloud; ticketing and the inference gateway adopted, not built ([ADR-0012](../adrs/ADR-0012-ticketing-platform-adopt-not-build.md), [ADR-0005](../adrs/ADR-0005-model-gateway-and-provider-independence.md)); one business deployable ([ADR-0004](../adrs/ADR-0004-event-driven-backbone.md)) | Platform |
| R10 | Cameras stress animals or violate heritage rules | Low | Medium | Vet sign-off per enclosure; non-invasive sensors where cameras are not acceptable (A7, A10) | Welfare |
| R11 | Model drift as seasons/animals change | High | Medium | Input/output drift monitors; scheduled re-evaluation; retraining pipeline ([ADR-0008](../adrs/ADR-0008-ai-evaluation-and-production-monitoring.md)) | AI platform |
| R12 | Vendor lock-in on the chosen cloud | Medium | Medium | Open protocols (MQTT, OpenTelemetry, Parquet); containerised services; portable vision models; exit assessment in [ADR-0003](../adrs/ADR-0003-cloud-provider-selection.md) | Platform |
| R13 | The AI needs a second operations team to feed it — labelling, reviewing, curating — that the estate does not have | Medium | High | Human roles and workload table with hours per role and phase ([AI platform](../hld/ai-platform/README.md#humans-in-the-loop-who-does-what)); "label as a side-effect" rule — every routine decision is captured as a label, no standalone labelling job after the initial golden sets; workload per role tracked as a metric; Phase gates slip before staff are overloaded | AI platform |
| R14 | Backups exist but a restore has never been tried; a system of record is lost | Low | High | NFR-DR-1/2/3 with RPO/RTO per data class; quarterly restore drill of welfare records and ticketing export into staging (GD-13); edge buffer replicated across broker nodes | Platform |
| R15 | LoRaWAN AppKey extracted from a stolen or opened sensor and used to inject readings | Low | Medium | Per-device keys, never shared; tamper switch raises a critical event and the device is de-registered at the join server; secure-element sensors at visitor-accessible locations; anomaly on a single sensor never triggers a tier-0 alert alone ([ADR-0002](../adrs/ADR-0002-mqtt-and-cellular-backhaul.md)) | Platform |
| R16 | POS integration is wrong or incomplete: spend figures drift from the vendor's books, a terminal is unmapped, card data reaches an event | Medium | Medium | `PurchaseRecorded` as an auditable event with transaction id and type (FR-2.6); runtime PII deny-list in the anti-corruption layer (PAN/Luhn, e-mail, phone) → reject + incident; unknown terminal → "unmapped" bucket + alert; daily reconciliation against end-of-day totals, Δ > 1% marks spend "provisional"; game day GD-14 ([business data health](../appendix/data-health-and-verification.md#business-data-health)) | Ticketing |
| R17 | The estate's physical capacity binds before the software does — parking and lunch seating in year 1 on the model's assumptions; egress, toilets, kitchens and access roads not assessed at all; growth stalls or the peak-day experience collapses | High | High | Capacity verdict and "what the estate must fund" table in [08 §1 and §5](../appendix/business-case-model.md#1-capacity-reality-check) with owners and orders of magnitude; timed-entry slots, a daily cap and pass-holder reservations on capacity-managed days (FR-1.7); "days until parking binds" on the daily report; site survey in Phase 0 (A14) covering the unassessed constraints first | Estate |
| R18 | New-audience growth (≈ ×1.8 unique households — ≈ 3.2M households of catchment at 20% yearly penetration) does not materialise because it depends on marketing and on a market that may not be there | Medium | High | Stated as an external dependency with its own cost line and a catchment sanity check ([08 §5](../appendix/business-case-model.md#5-what-the-estate-must-fund-besides-the-platform)); the cohort model prints the new households needed each year; OKR 1.1 carries a base and a stretch so the gap is visible from month 1; the platform measures growth from day one and is credited with none of it | Countess |
| R19 | Passes cannibalise single-visit revenue: revenue per visitor-day falls faster than repeat visits rise | Medium | Medium | The trade is modelled explicitly (08 §2: pass share from the cohort model, not a convention); OKR 1.4 is revenue per visitor-day with revenue per unique household as an estimated second line; business guardrails on spend per visitor-day and on the pass share of admissions (A15); pass price is a yearly management decision, not a model output | Management |
| R20 | The platform's contribution to attendance cannot be proven, so the payback case is disputed | High | Medium | Attribution stated as a sensitivity in incremental visitor-days, never as a claim (08 §4); the only clean measurements are the S4 nudged-vs-control cohort and the S5 discounted-vs-control blocks; requirements/08 re-issued with measured values at Phase 2 entry and yearly (08 §6) | Platform |
| R21 | Indirect prompt injection: a poisoned knowledge-base document or a visitor message is obeyed as an instruction, and an agent uses a tool it should not | Medium | High | Retrieved content and visitor text enter the model as quoted data, never as the system or tool-selection prompt; tools come only from a typed whitelist so content cannot invent one; every effectful call needs a human to commit it; the attempt surfaces as a tool-error and an audit entry ([ADR-0013](../adrs/ADR-0013-role-agents-on-typed-tools.md)); game day GD-17 | AI platform |
| R22 | An agent writes derived "memory" that quietly degrades later decisions — a tampered norm, a standing exception — and the loop reinforces itself | Low | High | No agent-generated writes at all before Phase 3; when they arrive, only typed derivatives through a write-guard (schema, ranges, provenance, dedup), critical derivatives human-approved, PSI on self-generated writes, and rollback of a batch by provenance ([ADR-0013](../adrs/ADR-0013-role-agents-on-typed-tools.md) §7) | AI platform |
| R23 | An agent task runs away — a tool loop, a retry storm, or a chain that keeps escalating — and costs money or duplicates an action | Medium | Medium | Step and token budget per task with degrade-and-escalate on exhaustion (NFR-AGT-1); effectful tools idempotent on a business key so a retry is harmless; cost per task in the same per-capability budget as everything else ([ADR-0005](../adrs/ADR-0005-model-gateway-and-provider-independence.md) §5); game days GD-18 and GD-20 | AI platform |
